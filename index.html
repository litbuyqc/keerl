Oopbuy shopping guide

A calmer route from browse to decision.

Start with your needs, collect comparable information and resolve questions before paying.

01 / Collect

Choose the category.

Use the links above to explore CAFANS. For Oopbuy fashion finds, narrow your search by garment type, intended use, fit and budget before collecting individual listings.

02 / Compare

Match like with like.

When reviewing Oopbuy clothing finds, compare measurements and fabric details. For Oopbuy shoe finds, record foot length guidance, width information and the seller’s size chart.

03 / Verify

Close the information gaps.

Confirm the selected variant, current price, return conditions and shipping estimate with the relevant seller or service. Recheck older notes before relying on them.

Budget formula: item price + domestic delivery + applicable service fees + international shipping + applicable taxes or duties = estimated total. Convert amounts to one currency and keep a separate allowance for uncertain charges.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a comparison sheet used to organize product links and shopping notes. Useful fields include category, seller, price, measurements, materials, quality-control photo notes and estimated total cost.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides a comparison guide and direct CAFANS category links. It does not host a downloadable spreadsheet or live Oopbuy inventory.

How should I compare clothing sizes?

Compare the seller’s measurements with a garment you already own. Check the measurement units and method rather than relying only on labels such as small, medium or large.

Can quality-control photos prove authenticity?

No. Photos can help reveal visible condition, construction and measurement details, but they do not independently prove authenticity. Seek verifiable provenance when authenticity matters.

What should I include in the total cost?

Account for the item price, domestic delivery, any applicable service fees, international shipping and any applicable taxes or duties. Confirm current charges and destination rules before paying.

Is this an official Oopbuy or brand website?

No. This is an independent guide linking to CAFANS. It is not an official Oopbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès website.
